How to read and move around this graph

Circle = concept. Arrow = relationship. Read a connection from its first concept, along the arrow label, to the next concept. The layout is for readability; distance and position are not scores.

  • Find a concept: filters names and keeps their immediate neighbors. It does not call a model.
  • Background: includes or hides added background facts. Their origin is not a guarantee of truth.
  • Focus on selected: shows one concept and its neighbors. Use Show all to reset the search and filters.
  • Fit graph: brings all currently visible circles into view. Drag empty space to move, drag a circle to arrange it, or scroll to zoom.
  • Details: select a circle or arrow to read its meaning, origin and any saved evidence.

Select a circle or arrow

Its meaning and saved evidence will appear here. Blue means extracted from the document; orange means added background knowledge; purple means a professor manual edit; pink means a validated student contribution.

Document knowledge is model-extracted from your text. Background knowledge is model-generated enrichment. Colors describe origin, not correctness. Scores are not calibrated probabilities.

Select a node or arrow for details. Drag to arrange or pan; scroll to zoom. Dotted lines identify additional participants in an assertion, not extra factual relationships. Search includes participants and immediate neighbors. Document knowledge takes precedence when a concept also appears in background additions.
